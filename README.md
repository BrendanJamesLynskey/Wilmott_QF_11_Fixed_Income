# Fixed-income Products, Yield and Swaps

Deck 11 of the [Paul Wilmott Introduces Quantitative Finance &mdash; Companion Series](https://github.com/BrendanJamesLynskey/Wilmott_QF_Hub).

**Live presentation:** https://brendanjameslynskey.github.io/Wilmott_QF_11_Fixed_Income/

A guided tour of chapters 14 and 15 of *Paul Wilmott Introduces Quantitative Finance*
(2nd edition, Wiley, 2007) &mdash; bonds, the yield curve, duration and convexity,
bootstrapping, and the vanilla interest-rate swap.

## What's inside

- Zero-coupon and coupon bonds; the money-market account and STRIPS
- Forward rate agreements (FRAs) and repos
- Day-count conventions, accrued interest and the clean/dirty price split
- Continuously vs. discretely compounded yield $y$
- The yield curve: spot, forward and par rates &mdash; three views of one object
- Macaulay and modified duration; DV01
- Convexity and the second-order Taylor correction
- Bootstrapping the discount curve from market prices, plus interpolation methods
- Vanilla interest-rate swaps: $K^* = (1 - Z(0,t_n)) / \sum_i \tau_i Z(0,t_i)$
- **Interactive bond yield, duration &amp; convexity calculator** &mdash; drag coupon, maturity, face and yield sliders and watch price, modified duration, convexity, DV01, the price-yield curve, the Taylor approximations and the cashflow schedule update live
